Common Emergencies

Bleeding

Symptoms: Visible blood loss, pain at injury site, weakness/dizziness.

Steps:

  1. Apply direct pressure using a clean cloth/bandage.
  2. Elevate the injured area if possible.
  3. Continue applying pressure until bleeding stops.
  4. Seek medical attention if severe.

Burns

Types: 1st, 2nd, and 3rd-degree burns.

Steps:

  1. Cool burn under running water for 10+ mins.
  2. Remove jewelry or tight items nearby.
  3. Cover with sterile, non-stick dressing.
  4. DO NOT apply ice, butter, or toothpaste.

Choking

Symptoms: Difficulty breathing, inability to speak, clutching throat.

Steps:

  1. Encourage coughing if able.
  2. Give five back blows between shoulder blades.
  3. Perform abdominal thrusts (if trained).
  4. Call emergency services if airway remains blocked.

Fractures

Symptoms: Severe pain, swelling, deformity, difficulty moving limb.

Steps:

  1. Keep the injured area still.
  2. Immobilize the limb using a splint if available.
  3. Apply a cold compress to reduce swelling.
  4. Seek immediate medical attention.

Fainting

Symptoms: Sudden loss of consciousness, dizziness, pale skin, weak pulse.

Steps:

  1. Lay the person flat on their back.
  2. Raise the legs slightly.
  3. Loosen tight clothing and allow fresh air.
  4. Seek medical evaluation if they don't wake promptly.

References: World Health Organization | International Federation of Red Cross | American Red Cross
