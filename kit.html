Emergency Preparedness

The Essential
First Aid Kit

Every household, school, and workplace should have a well-stocked first aid kit readily available. Disasters and accidents don't schedule appointments.

Having the right supplies within arm's reach allows for an immediate response to minor injuries and helps prevent severe complications while waiting for medical care.

Always Ready

Check your kit supplies
every 6 months.

The Master Checklist

Build your emergency response kit using these four essential categories to ensure you are prepared for any situation.

Bandages & Dressings

  • Adhesive bandages (various sizes for minor cuts)
  • Sterile gauze pads (for larger wounds)
  • Elastic bandage wraps (for sprains or strains)
  • Triangular bandages (to create slings)

Medical Tools

  • Medical tape (to secure gauze and dressings)
  • Trauma scissors (to safely cut away clothing)
  • Tweezers (for removing splinters or debris)
  • Digital thermometer (for checking fevers)

Sanitation & Safety

  • Antiseptic wipes (to clean wounds instantly)
  • Disposable nitrile gloves (minimum 2 pairs)
  • Face masks / CPR face shield (for protection)
  • Hand sanitizer (minimum 60% alcohol)

Relief Supplies

  • Instant cold packs (to reduce sudden swelling)
  • Burn gel or aloe vera cream (for minor burns)
  • Pain relievers (Ibuprofen or Acetaminophen)
  • Anti-itch lotion (for insect bites or rashes)

References: World Health Organization | IFRC | American Red Cross
